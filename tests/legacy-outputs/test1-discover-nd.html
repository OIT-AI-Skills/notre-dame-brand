How to Apply

Three steps to your first placement

Discover ND matches students with faculty by interest, skill, and timing. Most placements begin in the fall or spring semester following application.

  1. Browse projects

    Open opportunities are posted by faculty across every college. Filter by department, time commitment, or stipend availability.

  2. Submit one application

    A single application sends your interest to up to three projects. Include a short statement of interest and a current transcript.

  3. Meet your faculty mentor

    Faculty review applications and reach out to schedule conversations. Placements are confirmed within four weeks of submission.
